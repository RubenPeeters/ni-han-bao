# Link your accounts

Your Discord account is your login on the site. A League of Legends account is optional.

## Log in on the site

1. Press **Log in with Discord** at the top of any page, and allow it on Discord's screen.
2. You land on **Your servers**: every server you share with the bot. Pick one to see its pages.

![Your servers, after logging in](/docs/shots/servers.png)

Each server's pages are for its members only. Someone who is not in the server gets a "not found" page.

**If your server is not in the list** (or you see "The bot is not in any of your servers yet"): check the bot is in that server; [add it](/docs/get-started/add-the-bot) if you can. Just added or just joined? Give the bot a minute to see you, then reload. After `/forget-me`, use `/show-me` first.

## Your profile

Open the account menu (your avatar, top right) and pick **My profile**. It shows your activity in every server you are in, and your Riftbound collection, loans and wishlist, as others see them. To manage those, use **My collection**, **My loans** and **My wishlist** under **Riftbound**.

![A profile page](/docs/shots/profile.png)

Who can see it is up to you, with [`/profile visibility`](/docs/commands#profile) or under **Profile** on [Settings](/settings#profile) (account menu, **Settings**):

- **Private**: only you. This is the default.
- **Server members**: people who share a server with you, for the servers you share.
- **Public**: anyone logged in to the website.

To leave the server pages' lists and charts entirely, use `/hide-me`; `/show-me` brings you back.

## Link a League of Legends account

1. Run `/lol link riot-id:GameName#TAG region:Europe West`, with your Riot ID as the Riot client shows it.
2. The bot asks you to set a starter profile icon in League, to prove the account is yours:

<ReplyExample name="lol-link" />

Set that icon in the League client, wait a minute, and press **Verify**.

**What you should see**: "Linked: … is your League account." Your rank then shows on your profile and on the server's **Ranked** page, and your games in the weekly report.

**If you don't**:

- **"Your icon is not … yet"**: the icon can take up to 5 minutes to reach Riot's servers. Press Verify again a bit later.
- **"There is no Riot account named …"**: check the name and the `#TAG`.
- **"… has no League account on …"**: pick the region you play on.
- **"Timed out"**: Verify works for 14 minutes. Run `/lol link` again.

`/lol unlink` removes the linked account. You can change your icon back once you are linked.

## Delete your data

`/forget-me` deletes everything the bot stores about you, in every server: your counts, name and avatar, linked account, collection, loans and settings. It asks you to confirm first and cannot be undone. The bot then stops counting you until you use `/show-me`, and you cannot open the servers' pages until then.
