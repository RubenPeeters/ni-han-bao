# Riftbound

Look up cards, keep your collection, and lend cards to the people you play with. The cards are Riot's own, from the official card gallery.

## Look up a card

`/card name:` and a card's name, a legend's champion, or a collector code like `OGN-255`. Pick one from the suggestions as you type.

<DiscordShot name="card-autocomplete" alt="/card with suggestions while typing" />

**What you should see**: the card's first printing, its other versions underneath, Previous and Next to page through them for 15 minutes (a click after that removes them), and a link to its page on the site:

<ReplyExample name="card" />

**If you don't**: "No card called or numbered …" means nothing matched; pick from the suggestions. "More than one card matches" lists them, so you can be more precise.

## The Cards page

**Riftbound** in the site's header opens **Cards**, which lists every card, with search and filters for type, domain, cost and set. A card's page shows its printings, and under **Who has it?** the members of your servers with copies to lend.

![The Cards page](/docs/shots/cards.png)

## Your collection

On the Cards page, press **Edit my collection**, then count copies with the steppers on each card. **What the steppers count** switches between **Owned**, **For trade** and **To lend**; condition and language apply to the copies you add. Changes save as you go: "All changes saved".

From Discord, `/collection add card:Ahri count:2` adds copies too, in the language you last used unless you pick one. The reply shows the card and your new count.

<ReplyExample name="collection-add" />

**My collection**, under **Riftbound**, shows the cards you own with the same steppers, and sums them up per set, rarity and language. Others see those totals on your profile's **Collection** tab; who sees it follows your [profile visibility](/docs/get-started/link-accounts#your-profile).

![My collection](/docs/shots/collection.png)

`/collection share` posts a summary in the channel: how many cards you own, per set, your five rarest, and a link to your profile's **Collection** tab. `/wishlist share` does the same for the cards you still want, the first ten by name: those for your decks and cards you added first, then the playset goal's. Both follow your [profile visibility](/docs/get-started/link-accounts#your-profile): for **Server members**, the reply says the link opens only for members of your servers; a **Private** profile posts nothing and tells only you what to change.

### Your wishlist

**My wishlist**, under **Riftbound**, lists the cards you still want in two sections:

- **For my decks and cards I added**: what the decks that count on your wishlist need, and the cards you add by hand, with how many you want in all. These come first.
- **Playset goal**: tick a set under **Playset goal** (or **All sets**, which also covers the sets still to come) to want 3 copies of each of its cards, in any printing and language. Only the set's normal numbers count, as with **I own a playset**: no alternate art, starred, overnumbered, SP, token or rune printings. A card printed in two of your sets wants 3, not 6.

The copies you own go to your decks first, then to the cards you added, then to the playset goal, so the goal never wants copies on top of the decks. A card can be in both sections, with its split: "2 for decks, 1 for the playset".

On a server's **Trades** page, playset goal cards come below your other matches. Only the first section gets a DM from the bot or an item on the home feed when someone has a card for trade.

`/card`, `/deck share`, `/collection` and `/wishlist share` work in DMs too once you [add nexus to your account](/docs/get-started/add-the-bot#use-it-in-dms).

## Lending

Lending is off until you turn it on, per person:

- Under **Riftbound** on [Settings](/settings#riftbound), tick **I lend cards**, and choose what you lend: **Nothing until I mark cards** (then set **To lend** per card) or **Everything I own**, and press **Save**.
- Or in Discord: `/collection lending state:On: I lend cards`.

Members of your servers then see your copies to lend under **Who has it?** on a card's page. Your profile must not be private for that.

To record a loan: `/lend member:@Chloé cards:2 Jinx; Ahri`, with an optional `due` date. The borrower gets **Confirm** and **Dispute** buttons in the channel. When the cards are back, either of you runs `/return member:@Chloé`. Lost cards: the owner runs `/return` with `lost:True`.

<ReplyExample name="lend" />

To lend a whole deck as one loan, with one due date and one confirmation: `/lend member:@Chloé deck:Ahri control`, or **Lend deck** under **Lending** on the deck's page. A loan made on the site is confirmed on the borrower's **My loans** page. Tick **Not for lending** on a deck you keep built: its cards are then never offered to lend, whatever your per-card counts say.

A deck's page lists, under **Who has my missing cards?**, the members of your servers with copies to lend of each card you are missing.

Both lists show copies in the languages you accept when borrowing (English unless you change it on **Settings**) first, and the others as **also**. A deck that takes only its own language uses that instead.

**My loans**, under **Riftbound**, shows who has your cards, what you owe, and what is overdue, unconfirmed or disputed.

![My loans](/docs/shots/loans.png)

**If you don't** see a member's cards to lend: they have not turned lending on, their profile is private, or they are not in a server with you.

## Sharing a deck

`/deck share deck:Ahri control` posts one of your decks in the channel: pictures of its Legend and Chosen Champion, its energy curve, how many cards its main deck has, and a link to its page with the full list. **Copy as text** under it sends anyone who presses it the deck as `3 Card Name` lines, only for them. A private deck asks first whether your servers' members or anyone logged in may see it.

<ReplyExample name="deck-share" />

A public deck's link shows its Legend and Chosen Champion to anyone; the full list is for whoever logs in with Discord. **Copy as text** on a deck's page copies it as `3 Card Name` lines, which **Or paste a list** reads back into any deck.
