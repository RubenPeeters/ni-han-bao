# Riftbound

Look up cards, keep your collection, and lend cards to the people you play with. The cards are placeholders for now, until Riot's card data is available.

## Look up a card

`/card name:` and a card's name, a legend's champion, or a collector code like `PLH-L001`. Pick one from the suggestions as you type.

<DiscordShot name="card-autocomplete" alt="/card with suggestions while typing" />

**What you should see**: the card, with a link to its page on the site:

<ReplyExample name="card" />

**If you don't**: "No card called or numbered …" means nothing matched; pick from the suggestions. "More than one card matches" lists them, so you can be more precise.

## The Cards page

**Cards** in the site's header lists every card, with search and filters for type, domain, cost and set. A card's page shows its printings, and under **Who has it?** the members of your servers with copies to lend.

![The Cards page](/docs/shots/cards.png)

## Your collection

On the Cards page, press **Edit my collection**, then count copies with the steppers on each card. **What the steppers count** switches between **Owned**, **For trade** and **To lend**; condition and language apply to the copies you add. Changes save as you go: "All changes saved".

From Discord, `/collection add card:Ahri count:2` adds copies too, in the language you last used unless you pick one.

Your profile's **Collection** tab sums it up per set, rarity and language. Who sees it follows your [profile visibility](/docs/get-started/link-accounts#your-profile).

![A collection](/docs/shots/collection.png)

## Lending

Lending is off until you turn it on, per person:

- On your **Collection** tab, tick **I lend cards**, and choose what you lend: **Nothing until I mark cards** (then set **To lend** per card) or **Everything I own**, and press **Save**.
- Or in Discord: `/collection lending state:On: I lend cards`.

Members of your servers then see your copies to lend under **Who has it?** on a card's page. Your profile must not be private for that.

To record a loan: `/lend member:@Chloé cards:2 Jinx; Ahri`, with an optional `due` date. The borrower gets **Confirm** and **Dispute** buttons in the channel. When the cards are back, either of you runs `/return member:@Chloé`. Lost cards: the owner runs `/return` with `lost:True`.

To lend a whole deck as one loan, with one due date and one confirmation: `/lend member:@Chloé deck:Ahri control`, or **Lend deck** under **Lending** on the deck's page. A loan made on the site is confirmed on the borrower's **Loans** tab. Tick **Not for lending** on a deck you keep built: its cards are then never offered to lend, whatever your per-card counts say.

A deck's page lists, under **Who has my missing cards?**, the members of your servers with copies to lend of each card you are missing.

The **Loans** tab of your profile shows who has your cards, what you owe, and what is overdue, unconfirmed or disputed.

![The Loans tab](/docs/shots/loans.png)

**If you don't** see a member's cards to lend: they have not turned lending on, their profile is private, or they are not in a server with you.
