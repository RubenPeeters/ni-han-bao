# Privacy and leaving

The Demo server's members see each other's names and counts. This last page is about choosing how much of that you share, and how to leave.

## What is stored

The bot stores counts, times, ids and names: how many messages, in which channel, at which hour; reactions; time in voice; games from your Discord status; joins and leaves. It never stores what anyone writes: it does not even have Discord's permission to read message content. League ranks and games are stored only after `/lol link`, Riftbound cards only when you enter them. [About & privacy](/about) has the full list.

Logged out, the site shows totals across all servers only: no server names, nobody's name. The Demo server is not even in those totals, since Anna [left it out](/docs/tutorial/new-server#choosing-the-settings).

## Your profile

Anna's profile brings her servers together: her activity, her ranks, her collection and her loans. Who sees it is hers to choose, on [Settings](/settings#profile) or with `/profile visibility`:

- **Private**: only you. Every profile starts here.
- **Server members**: people who share a server with you, for the servers you share. Anna picked this, so her members can see her collection.
- **Public**: anyone logged in.

![A profile page](/docs/shots/profile.png)

A private profile also keeps you out of **Playing now** and out of **Who has it?** on card pages.

## Hiding in one server

Hana does not want to be on the Demo server's leaderboard. She types `/hide-me` there. She is gone from that server's lists and charts, and her rank role is taken away. Her messages still count towards the server's totals, without her name. `/show-me` brings her back. It works per server, so she runs it in each server she wants to hide from.

## Forgetting everything

Milan is leaving for good. `/forget-me` deletes everything the bot stores about him, in every server. It asks first, and only he sees the question:

He presses **Delete my data**. Server totals keep their counts, without anything pointing to him. The bot stops counting him and keeps only a hashed copy of his Discord id, to know not to. Until he types `/show-me`, he cannot open the servers' pages on the site.

## Removing the bot

To remove the bot from a server, an admin kicks it like any member, or removes it under Server Settings > Integrations. It stops counting at once, and the server leaves everyone's server list. To have its statistics deleted as well, ask through [About & privacy](/about).

That is the whole tour. The [command reference](/docs/commands) has every command and option.
